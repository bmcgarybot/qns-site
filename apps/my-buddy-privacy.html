My Buddy AAC - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 7, 2026

Summary: My Buddy AAC does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Overview

My Buddy AAC is an augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) app designed for individuals with speech and language difficulties. It is built by BMC Luminary Ventures LLC, Prescott, Arizona.

We take privacy seriously - especially because this app may be used by children and vulnerable individuals.

Data Collection

We collect no data whatsoever.

  • No personal information is collected
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No cookies (web version uses localStorage only)
  • No third-party SDKs, libraries, or frameworks
  • No network requests - the app works 100% offline

Data Storage

All app data - including the child's name, address, emergency info, custom words, and preferences - is stored locally on your device only using the browser's localStorage API. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is never transmitted to any server
  • Is never shared with any third party
  • Can be cleared at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings

Camera & Photo Library

The app may request access to your device's camera or photo library so you can use personal photos as picture icons for words. Photos selected are stored locally on the device and are never uploaded or transmitted anywhere.

Speech / Text-to-Speech

My Buddy AAC uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine (Web Speech API) to speak words and sentences aloud. All speech processing happens on-device. No audio data is recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

My Buddy AAC is designed to be safe for use by children. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • We do not collect any personal information from children or adults
  • We do not require account creation or login
  • We do not use any form of tracking or behavioral profiling
  • The app contains no in-app purchases, ads, or links to external websites

Third-Party Services

My Buddy AAC uses zero third-party services. There are no analytics providers, crash reporters, advertising networks, or cloud services integrated into this app.

Open-Source Symbols

The app includes pictographic symbols from the Mulberry Symbols project, licensed under CC BY-SA 2.0 UK. These are static image files bundled with the app - no network requests are made to fetch them.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this privacy policy, we will update this page with the new effective date. Given that this app collects no data, we do not anticipate changes.
