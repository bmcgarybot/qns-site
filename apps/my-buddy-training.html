A simple training order

  1. Complete the app's built-in tour and enter only the personal safety information you want stored on that device.
  2. Print the backup boards and keep them with the device.
  3. Read the Caregiver Guide and give regular communication partners the Partner Guide.
  4. Choose one practice or routine board that matches a real activity that day.

Important: My Buddy is not a medical device or a substitute for an individualized AAC assessment or speech-language pathologist. Keep a backup way to communicate.
