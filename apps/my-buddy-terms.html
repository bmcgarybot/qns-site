What My Buddy Is

My Buddy AAC is a free augmentative and alternative communication tool published by BMC Luminary Ventures LLC of Prescott, Arizona. It gives a person a grid of words and symbols and speaks them aloud using the device's own text-to-speech.

It is free and will stay free. There is no cost, no advertising, no in-app purchase and no subscription.

Not a Medical Device

My Buddy is not a medical device. It has not been reviewed, cleared or approved by the United States Food and Drug Administration or by any comparable body in any other country. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition.

Not a Substitute for Professional Support

Choosing and setting up a communication system is clinical work. My Buddy is not a substitute for assessment, prescription, training or follow-up by a qualified speech-language pathologist, teacher or clinician. If you are choosing a communication system for a child or an adult who needs one, please involve a professional. We would rather someone use a different tool that a clinician chose for them than use ours because it was free.

Emergencies

My Buddy must not be relied on as the only way a person can summon help. Software can fail, a device can lose power, a screen can break, and speech output can be missed in a noisy room. Anyone who could face a medical or safety emergency needs a backup that does not depend on this app or on any single device.

The Emergency button in the app speaks information that you typed in yourself. It does not contact emergency services, it does not send a message to anyone, and it does not alert us. It only speaks aloud.

Accuracy of Speech Output

My Buddy builds some phrases automatically from the word you select. We check this work and correct it when we find mistakes, but automatically generated language can still be wrong, awkward, or not what the person meant. The voice quality, pronunciation and accent come from your device, not from us. Always confirm that you have understood what someone is trying to say rather than relying on the app's wording alone.
