What exactly is Quantum Neuroshift?

+

Nervous system work for people stuck in repeating patterns. Anxiety loops, shutdown, pain flare-ups, sleep disruption, emotional reactivity. We find the pattern your body runs under pressure, work with it using guided regulation, somatic tapping, hypnosis, NLP, and EFT, and practice a steadier response. It's not talk therapy and it's not meditation — it's coaching aimed at the automatic pattern rather than the story about it.

How is this different from therapy?

+

Therapy typically works top-down — you talk about the problem, build insight, develop coping strategies. That's real and valuable work. This is coaching, and it works bottom-up: with the automatic reaction itself, the thing that fires before your conscious mind gets a say. Different target, different tools. Plenty of people do both at the same time, and I'm glad when they do.
