01 / WHEN YOU GO TO BED

Turn out the light. Get some sleep.

Picture lying down without starting the argument again in your head. No trying out the words you wish you’d said. No getting angry all over again. Just being tired, closing your eyes, and letting the night be a night.

02 / WITH THE PEOPLE YOU LOVE

Be in the conversation you’re having now.

Your partner is telling you about their day. Your kid wants to show you something. You hear them. You laugh. You aren’t halfway through a conversation with somebody who isn’t even in the room.

03 / WHEN THE MEMORY COMES BACK

Remember it without losing the rest of your day.

You see their name. You pass that place. Something reminds you. What would it be like to remember, then keep making dinner, finish your work, or enjoy the afternoon instead of spending it back there?

04 / FOR WHAT YOU DID

Own it. Repair what you can. Give yourself a chance.

You can take responsibility without making every good day feel like something you don’t deserve. An apology, a changed choice, a repair you can actually make. There may still be work to do. You deserve the chance to do it without tearing yourself apart every day.

05 / FOR WHAT THEY DID

Your next good day doesn’t need their permission.

They may never apologize. You may never speak again. You can keep your distance and still want their words to hurt less. Imagine hearing their name and being able to carry on with what you were doing.

06 / FOR WHAT YOU CAN’T TAKE BACK

Make room for what can still happen.

The chance you didn’t take. The last conversation. The thing you wish you’d done differently. You can care about what you lost and still say yes to dinner, a new opportunity, or somebody who wants to spend time with you now.

That is what we’re working toward.

Before you join

A few things
to know.

You choose what you share.
You choose whether you participate.

Is this only about forgiving myself?

No. For what you did. For what they did. For what you can’t take back. Bring the regret you hold against yourself or the hurt and anger you still feel toward someone else. You choose what you want to work on.

Do I have to forgive them or contact them?

No. You decide whether you want to explore forgiveness and what that means to you. You will not be asked to excuse harm, reconcile, contact someone, or give up a boundary.

Do I have to tell everyone what happened?

No. You can participate quietly without telling your story. Keep other people's names, identifying information and private details out of group posts and discussion. Passing, pausing and stopping are always allowed.

Is this therapy or medical treatment?

No. This is nonclinical coaching and personal-development education. It does not diagnose or treat mental-health or medical conditions, including trauma, PTSD or addiction, and does not replace psychotherapy, medical care or emergency services. No specific result is promised.

How do I attend, and when is it?

Join the free Quantum Neuroshift group on Skool. The workshop is Saturday, November 14, 2026, at 11 a.m. Arizona time (Mountain Standard Time): 10 a.m. Pacific, noon Central and 1 p.m. Eastern. The workshop runs for 90 minutes. Entry instructions will be posted inside the group. Joining now does not reserve a paid place.

How private is it? Will there be a recording?

Access is through the Skool community. Members are asked not to record, screenshot or share anyone else's disclosures. An online group cannot guarantee confidentiality, so keep identifying details private. Any workshop replay will contain presenter teaching only, not participant disclosures. The paid sessions will not be recorded.

What if I want to go further afterward?

I'll explain a separate paid experience: three live 90-minute group sessions to keep working on what you haven’t been able to let go of. Dates, pricing, support and purchase terms will be available before enrollment opens. No particular spiritual belief or inner experience is required.

Is there anything I should do before participating?

Choose a manageable everyday situation for the free demonstration. Participate from somewhere you can pay attention and safely pause. Do not do guided exercises while driving or drinking alcohol. This workshop is not crisis support. In an immediate emergency, contact your local emergency service.
