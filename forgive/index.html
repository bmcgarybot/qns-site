Before you join

Is this therapy or medical treatment?

No. Quantum Neuroshift provides nonclinical coaching and personal-development education. This workshop does not diagnose or treat mental-health or medical conditions and does not replace psychotherapy, medical care, or emergency services. No specific result is promised.

Do I have to forgive someone or reconnect with them?

No. You decide what forgiveness means for you and whether you want to explore it. You will not be asked to excuse harm, contact anyone, reconcile, or change your boundaries.

Will I have to tell everyone what happened?

No. You can participate without sharing your story. Avoid posting private incident details, names, or other identifying information in the group.

How private is the group?

Access is through the Skool community. Members are asked not to record, screenshot, or share another person's disclosures. An online group cannot guarantee confidentiality, so keep identifying details private. Any workshop replay will contain presenter teaching only, not participant disclosures.

What happens in the paid experience?

The planned continuation is three live 90-minute group sessions using EFT, hypnosis, NLP, visualization and integration practices. Exact dates, pricing, support and purchase terms will be shared before enrollment opens. The paid sessions will not be recorded. Spiritual interpretations are personal; no particular belief or experience is required.

When is the workshop?

We are planning a November workshop. The confirmed date, Arizona time, duration, access instructions and reminders will be posted in the group. Joining now does not reserve a paid place.

Your choices come first.

Participation is voluntary. Choose a manageable everyday situation for the demonstration, and pause or stop if you wish. Do not join guided exercises while driving, drinking alcohol, or doing something that requires your attention. This is not crisis support. In an immediate emergency, contact your local emergency service.
